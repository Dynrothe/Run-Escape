# Changelog

What's changed in Run Escape, newest first. The game shows this on its title screen too (click
the version in the corner), so it's written for players.

## 0.14.0 - 2026-10-29

### Added

- Four more maps after Varlamore, each opened by beating the one before, and each harder again.
  The Inferno is the hardest map in the game.
    - **Morytania**: Slepe and the swamp under Darkmeyer's walls, with Mort Myre's ghasts, shades
      and zombies, werewolves, vampyres, vyrewatch, nail beasts, mutated bloodvelds and the Theatre
      of Blood's Nylocas (the big ones split in two when they die). **Vanstrom Klause**, the
      **Pestilent Bloat** and **Sotetseg** come one by one, and at 15:00 **Verzik Vitur**:
      shielded on her throne at first, then up in her second form spitting webs and calling in
      Nylocas, and last as the great spider.
    - **God Wars Dungeon**: the main cave, where the armies of Bandos, Armadyl, Saradomin and
      Zamorak march in from their own sides and **fight each other** as much as they fight you.
      Lead them into each other: what they kill of each other is nobody's kill and drops nothing,
      but it's fewer of them. **Commander Zilyana**, **Kree'arra** and **K'ril Tsutsaroth** come
      one by one, each calling in their bodyguards as they're hurt, General Graardor leads an ork
      charge late on, and at 15:00 **Nex** comes in four forms (smoke, shadow, blood and ice),
      each calling in its own bodyguard.
    - **Prifddinas**: the elven city, with the Gauntlet's crystalline creatures, the city's guards,
      the Iorwerth, Zulrah's snakelings, and the corrupted creatures late on. **Zulrah** stays
      where it surfaces and dives into its next form twice, **Zalcano** brings the roof down, then
      the **Crystalline Hunllef**, and at 15:00 the **Corrupted Hunllef**, with tornadoes that
      chase you.
    - **The Inferno**: Mor Ul Rek and the Inferno's creatures: nibblers, bats, Jal-Ak blobs that
      split into three when they die, Jal-ImKot that dig up right next to you, Jal-Xil, and
      Jal-Zek that raise the dead. **JalTok-Jad** and his healers, a **Jal-Zek champion** who
      keeps raising them, and a **TzHaar-Ket champion** with an obsidian maul, who leaps onto
      you and calls in his own. At 15:00, **three JalTok-Jads at once**, each calling in healers:
      the run's won when the last of them falls.
- Two more **fun maps**, open from the start and endless:
    - **Castle Wars**: the Zamorak team in hooded cloaks and decorative armour, and the Saradomin
      team, who fight Zamorak as much as they fight you. Bosses: the **Flag carrier**, who runs
      off with your flag and teleports away when he's caught (two caskets if you get him), the
      **Catapult crew**, who drop rocks where you stood, and a **Zamorak tank** in full gold
      decorative armour.
    - **Pest Control**: the Void Knight's island, overrun by brawlers, ravagers, splatters that
      blow up when they die, shifters that teleport to you, defilers, torchers and spinners that
      heal the portals. The **four portals** open together, their shields dropping one by one,
      and call in pests until they're destroyed. Each drops a casket, and the last one a master
      casket too; a few minutes later the next four open.
- Ten more adventurers:
    - **Vampyre hunter** (Ivandis flail, hits bosses 25% harder): beat Morytania.
    - **Werewolf** (Dragon claws, heals off its hits now and then, moves 5% faster): kill the
      Pestilent Bloat in Morytania.
    - **Godsword knight** (a godsword, picks its hilt; takes 1 less damage, but weapons are 5%
      slower): beat the God Wars Dungeon.
    - **Armadyl ranger** (Armadyl crossbow, ranged hits 10% harder and flies 15% faster): kill
      Kree'arra in the God Wars Dungeon.
    - **Elf archer** (Crystal bow, moves 10% faster, ranged hits 5% harder): beat Prifddinas.
    - **Gauntlet runner** (Crystal staff, starts 30% weaker but hits 2% harder with every level,
      up to 50% harder at level 41): survive 10 minutes in Prifddinas.
    - **Infernal champion** (Toxic blowpipe, 20% more hitpoints): beat the Inferno.
    - **TzHaar-Hur** (Toktz-mej-tal, 15% bigger areas): kill JalTok-Jad in the Inferno.
    - **Castle Wars veteran** (Bronze 2h sword, bandages heal, 10% more hitpoints): kill the Flag
      carrier in Castle Wars.
    - **Void knight** (Bronze scimitar, in the Void knight's equipment, hits 10% harder): destroy
      the purple portal in Pest Control.
- Twelve more **quests**, three on each new map: Nature Spirit, Darkness of Hallowvale and A
  Night at the Theatre in Morytania; Temple of Ikov, The Frozen Door (all four generals) and
  Secrets of the North (Nex) in the God Wars Dungeon; Roving Elves, Regicide (Zulrah) and Song of
  the Elves (the Corrupted Hunllef) in Prifddinas; and in the Inferno, Nibblers, Begone!, Jad?
  What Are You Doing Here? and The Inferno itself, worth 2,500 coins for beating it. Crystalline
  rats count for Ratcatchers.
- **Quests in a run**: a quest tracker under the minimap shows how far this map's quests (and
  the ones done all over) have got, this run's kills included, coloured like the quest tab. Click
  its title or press Q to fold it away. The pause menu lists them with what each asks for.
- New class weapons, each brought in with its adventurer:
    - **Godsword** (Melee): a heavy two-handed sweep. At level 2 you **pick its hilt**, and with
      it its special attack: Armadyl, Bandos, Saradomin or Zamorak.
    - **Dragon claws** (Melee): four quick slashes at a time, the last the hardest.
    - **Ivandis flail** (Melee): vampyres and the undead are hit 50% harder.
    - **Armadyl crossbow** (Ranged): bolts that go through what they hit, and one in five is
      enchanted: a ruby's takes a share of what's left of its hitpoints, a diamond's goes on
      through more, a dragonstone's burns.
    - **Crystal bow** (Ranged): arrows split into three where they first hit.
    - **Crystal staff** (Magic): crystal spells that grow from basic to attuned at level 4 and
      perfected at 7.
    - **Toktz-mej-tal** (Magic): pulses of obsidian heat around you that leave the ground
      burning for a while.
- Every weapon can evolve now. New evolutions: the shortbow with **Rigour** into the **Dark
  bow**, the fire spells with **Augury** into **Flames of Zamorak**, the throwing axes with the
  Tome of fire into **Chain-hit axes**, Blood Burst with Rapid Heal into **Blood Barrage**, Smoke
  and Shadow Burst with the **Ancient sceptre** into **Smoke** and **Shadow Barrage**, the
  Ivandis flail with the Book of the dead into the **Blisterwood flail**, and the Crystal bow
  with the Graceful outfit into the **Corrupted bow**.
- Three new passives: **Rigour** (arrows, bolts and everything thrown hit harder), **Augury**
  (spells hit harder, and their effects last longer) and the **Ancient sceptre** (auras and
  Ancient spells cover more ground).
- **Gear sets**: wear enough pieces of a set for its bonus, shown on the casket and in your gear
  as "Void knight 2/4". Once you wear a piece, caskets often bring the set's missing ones, so a
  set started in a run can usually be finished in it. Torva, Virtus and the Zaryte vambraces come
  in once you've killed Nex.
    - **Void knight** (Uncommon; 3 of its 4 pieces): +10% damage.
    - **Inquisitor's** (Rare; 2 of 3): slashes and sweeps hit 15% harder.
    - **Crystal armour** (Rare; 2 of 3): crystal bows and staves hit 20% harder and are 15%
      faster.
    - **Torva** (Very rare; 2 of 3): 20% more hitpoints and 1 more armour.
    - **Virtus** (Very rare; 2 of 3): auras and Ancient spells cover 20% more area.
- More gear from caskets: the **Neitiznot faceguard**, the **Amulet of rancour**, the **Twisted
  buckler**, the **Zaryte vambraces** and Desert Treasure II's **Ultor**, **Venator**, **Magus**
  and **Bellator** rings. Some gear now boosts one style's damage (Melee, Ranged or Magic), or
  auras' area.
- Armoury lines for the new gear: the Void knight's into Elite void and its ornament kit, Crystal
  armour into corrupted crystal, Torva into Sanguine torva, Virtus into Ancient ceremonial, and
  the Amulet of rancour into the Amulet of rancour (s). A set's pieces still count for it once
  upgraded.

### Changed

- **Enemy projectiles are easier to see**: small ones are drawn bigger, and they all have an
  orange outline and a soft glow, so even arrows and bolts stand out.
- **Quests don't count on the fun maps**: kills and caskets there no longer go towards them, and
  the fun maps don't show the quest tracker.
- **The map list** is split into the campaign and the fun maps, and the map pointed at stays in
  view below the list, which scrolls on its own in a short window. Scrolling no longer swaps the
  map shown; only moving the pointer does. The adventurer list works the same way.
- The ring lines in the Armoury go one step further: the imbued Berserker, Seers and Archers
  rings become the **Ultor**, **Magus** and **Venator** rings.
- **Ice Barrage** counts as an Ancient burst now, like the other barrages, so it's the one
  Ancient spell you carry.
- Weapons no longer waste their shots on an enemy whose shield is up.
- When several bosses are up at once (Pest Control's portals), the boss bar shows the nearest.
  A final boss's bar stays up over the others.

### Fixed

- The minimap draws as soon as a run starts. On busy maps it could stay black for the first few
  seconds.

## 0.13.0 - 2026-10-29

### Added

- **Quests**, on the title screen: 22 of them, named for OSRS quests, that pay banked coins when
  they're done. Kill goblins for Goblin Diplomacy, Count Draynor for Vampyre Slayer, Fareed,
  Dessous and Damis for Desert Treasure I, Vorkath for Dragon Slayer II; open caskets for
  Pirate's Treasure. They count over as many runs as they take, and each pays once (100 to 1,500
  coins), when the run that finishes it ends. Kills and caskets count from this version on.
- **Amulet of blood fury**, a Rare amulet from reward caskets: +4% damage, and a 20% chance that
  a hit from any of your weapons heals you 2 hitpoints (one hit a second gets the chance). Upgrade
  it in the Armoury through Amulet of blood fury 2, 3 and 4, each likelier to heal than the last.

### Changed

- **Bones gather into piles**: bones lying close together slide into one pile worth all of their
  experience, so a crowd leaves a few piles instead of a carpet. A pile shows what it's worth as
  it grows, from Bones and Big bones up through Dragon, Wyvern and Lava dragon bones to Superior
  dragon bones. Coins gather into bigger stacks the same way.
- **Blood Burst heals more**: 5% of its hits heal you, up from 3%, and by 2 instead of 1.
- **The King Black Dragon is full size**: five tiles across like in OSRS, nearly twice as big as
  before. He stops with his heads at you instead of standing over you.
- **Old-school scroll bars**: menus that scroll get a bevelled brown bar in a dark track, like
  the old client's.

### Fixed

- Smoke, Shadow and Blood Burst sound like themselves instead of Ice Burst, and so do Damis's
  darkness, Skotizo's dark magic and the acid clouds of the Kalphite Queen, Vorkath and the
  Alchemical Hydra.

## 0.12.0 - 2026-10-28

### Added

- **Fun maps**, open from the start and endless from the start: no final boss, a new round every
  fifteen minutes, and bosses that turn up at random every few minutes instead of on a schedule.
  Everyone on them is another player, in real gear, and they talk.
    - **Grand Exchange**: spam bots, scammers in partyhats and gilded armour, lurers in team capes,
      gold farmers, fake moderators and merchants. Bosses: the Doubler (his trust trade takes some
      of your coins until he's dealt with), the Lure master and the Bot farmer.
    - **East Varrock**: the Wilderness, just past the ditch. Free-to-play pkers in multi, in
      nothing members get: clan noobs, rangers, mages, pures and tanks, and the ones coming up from
      Varrock jump the ditch to get at you. Bosses: a Clan leader who calls the pile, a Rune 2h
      pure and a Wildy mage.
    - **Falador**: 6 June 2006, world 111. You play Durial321, in Ahrim's robes and long white
      hair, with his whip, fire cape, obsidian shield and the green partyhat, while all of Falador
      begs to be killed and Massacre plays quietly in the background. Ur fault. Bosses: a Player
      moderator, Cursed You (in his trimmed Construction cape) and Mod Murdoch.
    - **Last Man Standing**: the Desert Island, everyone in LMS loadouts and chest loot. Bosses:
      an AGS specer, a Claws specer and a Dharok's sweat.
- Rangers and mages on the fun maps shoot back, a little. Rangers are rare, and their arrows are
  slow enough to step out of the way of.
- Four adventurers from the fun maps: the **Scammer** (survive 10 minutes on the Grand Exchange),
  the **F2P pker** (kill the Clan leader in East Varrock), **Durial321** (survive 6:06 in
  Falador) and the **LMS sweat** (kill the AGS specer in Last Man Standing).

### Changed

- The **Monk** is weaker: weapons are 15% faster, down from 25%. That bonus reached the weapon
  speed cap early and sped up every weapon the Monk picked up, not just Saradomin Strike.
- **Endless runs pay fewer coins** (fun maps included): half of what a normal run would, and
  less again each round as the crowds get bigger. Bigger crowds, more caskets and upgraded gear
  to alch made one long run worth dozens of normal ones.
- Endless rounds get harder a bit faster: enemies have 3.3x the hitpoints of the round before (up
  from 3x) and hit 1.7x as hard (up from 1.6x).

## 0.11.0 - 2026-10-28

### Added

- **Endless**: beat a map to play it endless, with the switch by the start button. Killing the
  final boss starts the next round instead of winning: the map comes round again from five minutes
  in, with more monsters that have three times the hitpoints, hit harder and leave better bones.
  It goes on until you fall, and each map keeps your furthest run.
- In endless runs, weapons and passives level on past their last level (Abyssal whip +3), and
  each level past the last makes the weapon hit harder still. Movement and weapon speed,
  projectiles, luck and effect duration stop where they are.
- Endless caskets bring gear upgraded once for each round after the first (a Slayer helmet +2 has
  twice its damage and armour). Food, potions, chinchompas, cannons and thralls get stronger with
  the rounds too, and each round's final boss drops a master casket.
- **Armoury**, on the title screen: upgrade the gear you've found in caskets for good, with your
  banked coins. Each upgrade takes a piece a step along its line, and from then on it comes out
  of every casket as what it's become: an Amulet of strength turns into an Amulet of power, then
  an Amulet of torture, and last an Amulet of torture (or). Lines follow OSRS where they can (the
  Fire cape to the Infernal cape, Recipe for Disaster's gloves, the boss boots) and end in
  ornament kits, imbues, Twisted ancestral, Masori (f) and the Avernic treads, each with a new
  look and a quarter more of its stats. The results screen says what's new in the Armoury after
  a run.

### Changed

- Scenery standing on its own only blocks where it stands: a signpost its post, a tree its trunk,
  a bush its middle. Scenery in rows and clumps blocks a little less than its tiles, so you no
  longer catch on any of it from a step away. Water, walls and fences block as before, and nothing
  can squeeze through anywhere it couldn't already.
- The world fades to black past the edge of the map, so you can see where it ends instead of
  walking into an invisible wall. A few blockers that weren't drawn at all are gone too.

### Fixed

- Bones left behind across the map no longer keep their experience for good once there are too
  many lying around: the furthest pile comes to where the newest bones fall instead.

## 0.10.0 - 2026-10-27

### Added

- Five more maps after the Wilderness, each opened by beating the one before: the Kharidian
  Desert, the Fremennik Province, Karamja, Kourend and Varlamore. Each has its own monsters, three
  mid-run bosses and a final boss: the Kalphite Queen, Vorkath, TzTok-Jad, the Alchemical Hydra
  and Sol Heredit.
- Ten more adventurers, two for each new map: the Nomad, Menaphite, Seer, Berserker, Obby mauler,
  Pirate, Pyromancer, Shayzien soldier, Gladiator and Javelineer.
- **Unlocks**: from the Desert on, beating a map or killing one of its bosses brings a weapon, a
  passive or a piece of gear into every run after. Each map lists its tasks and what they bring.
- Class weapons from the new maps: Osmumten's fang, the Tzhaar-ket-om, the Dragon warhammer and
  the Dual macuahuitl (Melee); the Dragon hunter crossbow and the Eclipse atlatl (Ranged); the
  Kodai wand and the Blue moon spear (Magic).
- The rest of the Ancient bursts: Smoke Burst poisons, Shadow Burst makes what it hits take 20%
  more damage and Blood Burst now and then heals you. You carry only one burst.
- Four evolutions: Astral runes, Toktz-xil-ul, Undead Grasp and the Colossal blade.
- **Rerolls**: one a run, and one more for each rank of the new Hunter skill. Use one on a level
  up's choices or a casket's item, with the button under them or R.
- **Export save** and **Import save** on the title screen, to keep a copy of your progress or
  take it to another browser.
- Thrownaxes (Common): thrown at the nearest enemy, they bounce on to more of them as they go from
  bronze to dragon.
- Elemental runes (Common): runes circle you for a few seconds, knocking back what they hit. Air
  to start, then water, earth and fire.
- The Toxic blowpipe (Rare, Ranged): darts that leave venom in what they hit. It aims for enemies
  without venom, so the venom spreads through a crowd.
- The Sanguinesti staff (Rare, Magic): blood blasts, and one hit in six heals you.

### Changed

- The Harmonised nightmare staff was far ahead of every other weapon against a crowd. It now casts
  every 0.8 seconds at first and every 0.7 at best (was 0.6 and 0.5), and its last level no longer
  brings faster casting, a bigger splash and pierce all at once.
- Antidote++ and Sanfew serum only turn up on maps where something poisons you.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-26

### Added

- The first playable version: survive fifteen minutes in Lumbridge, the swamp and Draynor against
  hordes of chickens, goblins, zombies and ghosts, until Count Draynor rises.
- Weapons that upgrade the OSRS way (scimitars, Fire Strike to Fire Wave, throwing knives),
  passives, evolutions and rarities.
- Levels on the OSRS experience table, with a choice of three upgrades each.
- Coins banked between runs for permanent skills, and an adventurer to unlock.
- Sound effects synthesized from the cache the way the client does it.

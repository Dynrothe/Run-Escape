### Android

Download `Run-Escape-<version>-android.apk` below and open it on your phone. The first time,
Android asks whether your browser may install apps: allow it.

The app doesn't update itself. For a new version, come back to the releases page and install its
APK over the one you have. Don't uninstall the old one first: that deletes your progress. To keep
a copy anyway, use Export in Settings.

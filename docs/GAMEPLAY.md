# How Run Escape plays

Everything in the game, map by map and system by system. The [README](../README.md) has the short
version and how to run it.

- **WASD** or the arrow keys to move. Weapons fire on their own. **1-4** use your utility items.
  **Esc** pauses. **Q** shows or hides the quest tracker. The mouse wheel zooms.
- Enemies drop **bones**; walk near them to collect their experience. Bones lying close together
  gather into one pile worth all of their experience, and it looks the part as it grows: Big
  bones, Dragon bones, Wyvern bones, Lava dragon bones, then Superior dragon bones. Coins gather
  into bigger stacks the same way. Levels follow the OSRS experience table (stretched for the
  later levels), and each one offers a choice of three: a new weapon, a weapon upgrade or a
  passive. **1-4** pick with the keyboard. **R** rerolls the offer (one reroll a run, more from
  the Pirate and the shop), and **X** skips it for coins from the run: 25 the first time, then
  50, 100, 200 and 400, doubling each time.
- When a run ends, the results screen shows its stats: damage, kills and damage a second for
  each weapon (and the utility items and power-ups that did damage), the biggest hit, damage
  taken, hitpoints healed and when each boss went down.
- You carry at most **4 weapons and 4 passives**, so a build is about what you leave out.
- **Rarity**, in OSRS drop table terms: every weapon and passive is Common, Uncommon, Rare or
  Very rare, which sets how often it's offered. Every offer also rolls its own rarity: a Common
  Piety adds 5% damage, a Very rare one 17.5%; rarer weapon upgrades add bonus damage to that
  weapon. Luck (Ring of wealth, the Slayer skill) makes rarer rolls likelier.
- **Weapons** upgrade the OSRS way: scimitars and 2h swords go bronze to dragon, Fire Strike
  becomes Bolt, Blast and Wave, throwing knives go bronze to rune, and shortbows go through the
  woods to magic while their arrows go through the metals. Thrownaxes go bronze to dragon, and
  each one bounces on from what it hits to the next enemy nearby, more times as they upgrade.
  Elemental runes circle you for a while, hitting what they pass and knocking it back, one more
  rune (air, water, earth, fire) every other level. A maxed scimitar with Piety evolves
  into the Abyssal whip; maxed Ice Burst with Protect from Melee becomes Ice Barrage. The weapon in your
  hand and passives like the Graceful outfit or Amulet of glory show up on your character.
- **The Ancient bursts**: Ice Burst freezes what's around you, Smoke Burst poisons it, Shadow
  Burst makes it take 20% more damage for a while and Blood Burst now and then heals you by 2.
  You carry only one burst, and they share one weapon's chance of being offered.
- **Evolutions** from the new maps' passives: maxed elemental runes with Vengeance become
  **Astral runes**, which never stop circling; maxed throwing knives with Obsidian armour become
  **Toktz-xil-ul**, obsidian rings that fly through everything; maxed Saradomin Strike with the
  Book of the dead becomes **Undead Grasp**, whose hands hold what they hit; and a maxed dragon 2h
  sword with Dizana's quiver becomes the **Colossal blade**, which hits bosses and elites again.
  The shortbow with **Rigour** becomes the **Dark bow**, the fire spells with **Augury**
  **Flames of Zamorak**, the thrownaxes with the Tome of fire **Chain-hit axes**, Blood Burst with
  Rapid Heal **Blood Barrage**, Smoke and Shadow Burst with the **Ancient sceptre** **Smoke** and
  **Shadow Barrage**, the Ivandis flail with the Book of the dead the **Blisterwood flail** and the
  Crystal bow with the Graceful outfit the **Corrupted bow**.
- **Class weapon evolutions**: the Scythe of Vitur with **Chivalry** becomes the **Sanguine Scythe
  of Vitur**, which sweeps wider and heals every fourth sweep; a godsword with **Piety** becomes the
  **Ancient godsword**, whose Blood Sacrifice hits all round and heals; the Dragon claws with the
  Tome of fire become the **Burning claws**; the Dragon warhammer with Protect from Melee becomes the
  **Elder maul**, whose Shockwave weakens everything around you; Dinh's bulwark with Vengeance becomes
  **Dinh's blazing bulwark**, whose Shield Bash reaches further and burns; the Twisted bow with Rigour
  becomes the **Corrupted twisted bow**; Tumeken's shadow with Augury becomes the **Corrupted
  tumeken's shadow**; the Sanguinesti staff with Rapid Heal becomes the **Holy sanguinesti staff**;
  and the Harmonised nightmare staff with the Amulet of glory becomes the **Eldritch nightmare staff**,
  whose surges split where they hit.
- **Evolving**: a weapon at its last level with its passive (at any level) offers its evolution first
  on every level up. An evolved weapon has three levels of its own and keeps the weapon's bonus
  damage. Most other class weapons, like the Toxic blowpipe and the Thunder khopesh, don't evolve.
  Level up cards say what evolves with what, the HUD and pause menu show how close a weapon is and
  its slot glows gold once it's ready, and the **Collection log** on the title screen lists every
  recipe, keeping what it makes as ??? until you've made it.
- **Style prayers**: **Chivalry** makes slashes and sweeps hit harder and adds max hitpoints for
  fighting up close, **Rigour** makes arrows, bolts and thrown weapons hit harder, and
  **Augury** makes spells hit harder and their effects last longer.
- **Adventurers**: three to start with, each with their own starting weapon, outfit and bonus.
  The rest join for beating a map or for a task done on one (in a single run, however it ends);
  the adventurer screen says what each one wants, and a task done mid-run rings the town
  crier's bell.

- **Class weapons**: every adventurer fights with Melee, Ranged or Magic, and only they can be
  offered their class's weapons. Each class has
  a Very rare one, offered in about one run in twenty, and Rare ones. A new weapon is always
  offered at its own rarity or better, so these come with bonus damage, and the rarest weapon
  you carry is the one in your hand.
    - **Melee**: the **Scythe of Vitur** sweeps a wide arc and hits bosses and elites three
      times (100%, 50%, 25%). The **godswords** are heavy two-handed sweeps, and every third
      swing that has something to hit is their special attack: Armadyl's The Judgement hits
      twice as hard, Bandos's Warstrike makes what it hits take 25% more damage, Saradomin's
      Healing Blade heals you by half the damage, and Zamorak's Ice Cleave freezes. You only
      carry one godsword, and the four share one weapon's chance of being offered. From the
      new maps: **Osmumten's fang**, fast stabs that hit bosses and elites again; the
      **Tzhaar-ket-om**, a slow sweep with huge knockback whose every fourth swing is a Quake
      that stuns; the **Dragon warhammer**, whose every third blow is a Smash that makes what it
      hits take 30% more damage; the **Dual macuahuitl**, very fast slashes on both sides; and
      the **Thunder khopesh**, quick slashes whose every third is a Thunderclap that hits
      everything around you; and **Dinh's bulwark**, a slow bash that hits everything around
      you, further out than any other melee weapon.
      The Godsword knight's **Godsword** is a bare blade until level 2, when you pick its hilt
      (Armadyl, Bandos, Saradomin or Zamorak) and with it its special attack. **Dragon claws**
      slash four times at once, the last the hardest, and the **Ivandis flail** hits vampyres and
      the undead 50% harder.
    - **Ranged**: the **Twisted bow** shoots the toughest enemy in range, and hits harder the
      more hitpoints it has (up to +150% on bosses). The **Bow of Faerdhinen** fires crystal
      arrows that fly on through what they hit. The **Toxic blowpipe** fires darts that leave
      venom in what they hit, hurting it every second; it goes for enemies without venom first
      (flying past the ones with it), so the venom spreads through a crowd. From the new maps:
      the **Dragon hunter crossbow**, heavy bolts that hit bosses and elites again, and the
      **Eclipse atlatl**, darts that fly through enemies and set them burning. The **Armadyl
      crossbow**'s bolts go through what they hit and one in five is enchanted (a ruby takes a
      share of what's left of its hitpoints, a diamond pierces more, a dragonstone burns), and
      the **Crystal bow**'s arrows split into three where they first hit.
    - **Magic**: **Tumeken's shadow** fires splashing shadow blasts and triples your damage
      bonus for them. The **Harmonised nightmare staff** casts Fire Surge faster than anything
      else. The **Sanguinesti staff** casts blood blasts, and one hit in six heals you for half
      its damage, up to 2 hitpoints. From the new maps: the **Kodai wand**, Water Surge at two
      enemies at once, and the **Blue moon spear**, ice that crashes down and freezes. The
      **Crystal staff** grows from basic to attuned (level 4) and perfected (level 7), and
      **Toktz-mej-tal** pulses obsidian heat around you that leaves the ground burning.
- **Unlocks**: from the Desert on, every map holds new things to find. Beating it, or killing
  one of its mid-run bosses, brings a weapon, a passive or a piece of gear into the game for
  every run after (a toast and the results screen say so). Until then it's never offered or
  dropped. Each map's card shows what it holds, faded until it's earned, and says on hover what
  to do for it. Everything from Lumbridge, the Barrows and the Wilderness is there from the
  start.

- **Fun maps**: the Grand Exchange, East Varrock, Falador, Last Man Standing, Castle Wars and Pest
  Control are open from the start and always endless. They have no final boss: a new round starts
  every fifteen minutes, tougher like an endless run's, and bosses come at random from each map's
  pool (the first at four minutes, then every three to five). Everyone there is another player,
  drawn in real gear (a few outfits per kind), and they say things over their heads; rangers and
  mages shoot, weakly. In Falador everyone plays Durial321, who answers back. Castle Wars has both
  teams, who fight each other too, and a Flag carrier who runs from you; Pest Control is the Void
  Knight's island and its pests, and its bosses are the four portals, which open together behind
  shields that drop one by one.
- **Late endless caskets**: past fifteen minutes in any endless run, fun maps included, a casket that
  would drop only has a 5% chance to, and only 10 can lie on the ground at once. A round's master
  casket skips the chance but not the limit. Past the limit you get "You have a funny feeling there
  would've been a chest".
- **Elites** (golden, bigger, about one a minute) drop **reward caskets**, and any enemy can
  drop one now and then. They come in clue scroll tiers (easy, medium, hard, elite, and master
  from bosses) and get better as the run goes on; a loot beam marks them, and an arrow at the
  edge of the screen points to ones out of view. Walk over one to open it: it rattles, bursts
  open and gives some coins and one item, which you equip, keep, use or high alch for coins.
- **Power-ups** drop now and then from anything that isn't a boss: the longer it's been since the
  last one, the likelier a kill drops one, so they turn up every couple of minutes early on and
  late. A cyan beam marks them; walk over one to use it there and then, before it blinks out
  after 30 seconds. Their damage keeps up with the enemies' hitpoints, and the same one never
  drops twice in a row.
    - **Dwarf multicannon**: sets up by you and shoots the nearest enemies for 20 seconds.
    - **Book of the Dead**: raises a Greater Ghost, Skeleton or Zombie thrall to fight beside you
      for 30 seconds. The ghost and skeleton shoot from your side; the zombie goes after things.
    - **Bot nuke**: everything on the map that isn't a boss dies (bosses lose a tenth of their
      hitpoints) and leaves its bones and drops behind.
    - **Magnet**: every drop on the ground flies to you, caskets and power-ups included.
    - **Giant stopwatch**: everything but bosses stands still for 8 seconds, even what turns up
      meanwhile.
    - **Sled**: you ride at 2.2 times your speed for 8 seconds and nothing can hurt you, poison and
      bosses included. It shoves enemies aside without hurting them, your weapons keep firing, and
      it frees you if you're frozen. Effect duration doesn't stretch it.
    - **Giant rune essence**: a moment after you pick it up, a huge rock lands on the thickest
      crowd within 12 tiles, marked in cyan. It hits everything within 5 tiles and throws
      survivors back, and bosses lose 8% of their hitpoints. It can't hurt you.
    - **Evil Bob**: a giant Evil Bob charges through the thickest crowd three times, over walls and
      all. Everything in his way is hit once a pass and thrown aside.
    - **Fairy ring**: a ring opens at your feet for 12 seconds. Anything that isn't a boss and comes
      within 2.5 tiles is warped far away, behind you if you're on the move. It stays where it
      opened, and shots still come through.
    - **Drill Demon**: "Run laps, maggots!" For 9 seconds everything within 12 tiles runs round you
      in a big circle, faster than usual, and can't attack, though shots already on their way still
      land. Bosses, what can't move and flag carriers carry on.
- **Maps**: every run is on one map, and beating a map (killing its final boss) opens the next.
  Enemies have more hitpoints and hit harder on each one. Pick where to go under the
  adventurers. Enemies only turn up where you could stand and walk to, never behind a wall or in
  rock, ghosts included (East Varrock's come from both sides of the ditch, which they jump). Each
  map's monsters are from its part of the world:
    - **Lumbridge**: chickens, rats, goblins, cows and imps, the swamp's giant rats and frogs,
      Draynor's dark wizards, and the zombies, skeletons, ghosts, spiders and bats of Draynor
      Manor.
    - **Barrows**: crypt rats, bloodworms and crypt spiders from the tunnels under the mounds
      and their skeletons, Mort'ton's afflicted and shades, ghouls and feral vampyres.
    - **Wilderness**: revenant imps, goblins, hobgoblins, pyrefiends and demons, bandits, dark
      warriors and chaos druids, the Graveyard of Shadows's zombies and skeletons, poison
      spiders, the Ankou and hill giants.
    - **Kharidian Desert**: desert lizards, scorpions, jackals, ugthanki, crocodiles, scarab
      swarms, the Bandit Camp's bandits, mummies and locust riders, and kalphite workers,
      soldiers and guardians.
    - **Fremennik Province**: rock crabs, wolves, yaks, cockatrices, basilisks, kurasks and
      jellies, mountain and ice trolls, sea snakes, rock lobsters, dagannoths and wallasalki.
    - **Karamja**: monkeys, snakes, jungle spiders, tribesmen, jogres, moss giants and lesser
      demons, and the TzHaar out of the volcano: Tz-Kih, Tz-Kek, Tok-Xil and Yt-MejKot.
    - **Kourend**: the Kebos swamp's guanic bats, swamp crabs, lizards and sulphur lizards,
      lizardmen and their brutes, bloodvelds, hydras, and Xeric's deathly rangers and mages and
      skeletal mystics.
    - **Varlamore**: embertailed jerboas, pyre foxes, antelopes, blood jaguars, serpent shamans
      and jaguar warriors, sulphur and frost naguas, the Fremennik warband, gryphons and
      minotaurs.
    - **Morytania**: Mort Myre's ghasts, shades and zombies, werewolves, vampyre juveniles and
      juvinates, vyrewatch, nail beasts, mutated bloodvelds and the Theatre of Blood's Nylocas
      (the big ones split in two).
    - **God Wars Dungeon**: the armies of Bandos, Armadyl, Saradomin and Zamorak, who march in
      from their own sides and fight each other as much as you: goblins, orks and cyclopes,
      aviansies, knights and priests, imps, hellhounds and bloodvelds, and the gods' spiritual
      warriors. Anything with a wall between it and the army it's after for 3 seconds gives up
      and comes for you for good; a crowd in the way or a long walk doesn't count.
    - **Prifddinas**: the Gauntlet's crystalline rats, bats, spiders, scorpions, wolves, bears
      and dark beasts, the city's guards, the Iorwerth's warriors and archers and Zulrah's
      snakelings, and the corrupted ones late on.
    - **The Inferno**: Mor Ul Rek's TzHaar, nibblers, Jal-MejRah bats, Jal-Ak blobs that split
      into three, Jal-ImKot that dig up next to you, Jal-Xil, and Jal-Zek that raise the dead.
- **Bosses** turn up during a run, each fighting the way it does in OSRS, and always drop an
  elite casket (now and then a master one). Big hits are wound up first, with a red circle
  showing where they'll land, and projectiles fly where you stood, so keep moving.
  At half their hitpoints every boss is **enraged**: it glows red, attacks more often, moves
  faster and brings out attacks it was holding back. The melee bosses (Count Draynor and the
  Barrows brothers but Karil and Ahrim) **leap** at anyone who keeps away: a red circle on the
  spot you're standing on, then they land on it.
    - **Lumbridge**: at 7:30 **Count Draynor**, with a bite that heals him, the bats of Draynor
      Manor (a swarm of them once he's enraged), and with no stake to finish him he gets back up
      once. At 15:00 the **Wise Old Man** of Draynor Village teleports in. He can't be fought in
      OSRS, so he fights the way the stories tell it, as a retired adventurer and wizard who once
      robbed the Draynor bank: Fire Wave, Ice Barrage on the spot you're standing on (it freezes
      you), a teleport away whenever you get close, Draynor's dark wizards called in to help,
      and Telekinetic Grab, which takes 5% of your coins (beat him and you get them back).
      Enraged, he casts Fire Wave three at a time and Ice Barrages all around you.
    - **Barrows**: the six brothers climb out of their crypts every two and a half minutes, each
      with his set effect. **Torag** (2:30) drains your run energy, slowing you; **Karil** (5:00)
      shoots from range and his tainted shot slows you too (enraged, he fires three bolts at
      once); **Guthan** (7:30) heals by what his warspear hits for; **Ahrim** (10:00) casts Fire
      Wave, Weaken and Confuse (and three Fire Waves at once, enraged); **Verac** (12:30) hits
      through your armour, leaps included; and **Dharok** comes last (15:00), hitting harder the
      lower his hitpoints get.
    - **Wilderness**: around the Bone Yard. At 5:00 the **Crazy archaeologist** (a crossbow, and
      Rain of knowledge: exploding books that land around you); at 10:00 the **Chaos Fanatic**
      (chaos magic, explosions around you, and a spell that knocks your weapons out of your
      hands); at 15:00 the **King Black Dragon**, with claws, dragonfire, poison breath, ice
      breath that freezes you and shock breath that drains your stats. Enraged, the archaeologist
      fires three bolts at once, the Fanatic's explosions come five at a time and the dragon
      breathes fire three ways.
    - **Kharidian Desert**: the Desert Treasure's guardians at 5:00, 7:30 and 10:00: **Fareed**
      (a fire scimitar, a fiery leap and Fire Wave), **Dessous** (a vampyre whose hits heal him)
      and **Damis**, whose Darkness weakens you and who gets back up once in his true form. At
      15:00 the **Kalphite Queen**: pincers, spines, poisoning acid and her hive; when her shell
      cracks she rises again airborne, casting hive magic.
    - **Fremennik Province**: the **Dagannoth Kings** one by one, Rex (bites and leaps), Supreme
      (thrownaxes from range) and Prime (Water Wave); at 15:00 **Vorkath**, with dragonfire, ice
      dragonfire that freezes you, acid that poisons and zombified spawn.
    - **Karamja**: **Elvarg** of Crandor (dragonfire and a firestorm), **Nezikchened** (fire
      blasts and demons called in) and **Ket-Zek** (magma bolts, and he teleports); at 15:00
      **TzTok-Jad**, who stomps, casts, drops rocks around you and calls in his healers.
    - **Kourend**: the **Lizardman shaman** (poisoning acid spit, a jump and spawn),
      **Sarachnis** (webs that freeze you, and spiderlings) and **Skotizo** (dark magic and
      reanimated demons); at 15:00 the **Alchemical Hydra**: lightning, poisoning acid pools and
      a firestorm.
    - **Varlamore**: the Fortis Colosseum's **Manticore** (claws, orbs and tail spikes), the
      **Shockwave Colossus** (lightning and shockwaves) and the **Javelin Colossus** (javelins

    - **Morytania**: **Vanstrom Klause**, the **Pestilent Bloat** and **Sotetseg**; at 15:00

    - **God Wars Dungeon**: **Commander Zilyana**, **Kree'arra** and **K'ril Tsutsaroth**, each
      calling in their bodyguards as they're hurt, and General Graardor's ork charge; at 15:00

    - **The Inferno**: **JalTok-Jad** with his healers, a **Jal-Zek champion** and a

- **Gear** goes in the OSRS equipment slots (head, cape, neck, body, shield, legs, hands, feet,
  ring) and shows on your character: about ten pieces a slot, from Rune and Mystic to the
  Barrows brothers' armour, Armadyl, Ancestral, the Infernal cape, Pegasian boots and an Avernic
  defender, and a Ring of recoil that hurts whatever hits you. One piece per slot; a new one
  replaces (and alchs) the old.
- **Gear sets**: the Void knight's equipment (3 of its 4 pieces), Inquisitor's, Crystal armour,
  Torva and Virtus (2 of their 3 each) give a bonus when enough pieces are worn, and once you
  wear one piece, caskets can bring the set's missing ones: 15% of the time, if the piece is no
  more than one rarity above what the casket rolled. Alch or reroll a piece of a set you've started
  and caskets stop bringing that set for the rest of the run.
- **Utility** items go in a 4 slot inventory and are used with **1-4** (or a click): sharks and
  anglerfish, potions (Super attack, strength and defence, Stamina, Magic, Ranging, Super
  combat, Bastion, Battlemage, Overload, Divine super combat) with a timer, brews (a Saradomin
  brew heals a lot and toughens you but weakens your hits; a Zamorak brew is the other way
  round), antidotes (Antidote++ and Sanfew serum cure poison and boss drains and curses, and
  keep them off for a while), a Bonecrusher that pulls in every bone on the ground, chinchompas that blow up
  everything around you, an Antique lamp worth a level. Copies stack.
- Coins and the odd cooked chicken drop too.
- Enemies get tougher as the run goes on, gently at first and faster after the first few
  minutes.
- At 15:00 the map's final boss comes. Kill it to win.
- **Minimap**: the client's, north up, drawn from the map's own minimap images, with the
  client's yellow dots for enemies (orange for elites), a red one for bosses, and caskets and
  power-ups in their colours. Bosses, caskets and power-ups out of range sit on the rim.
- **Hitsplats** are the client's own sprites: red for damage (on you too), green for poison.
- **Coins** are banked when a run ends. Spend them on **Skills** (permanent bonuses, refundable).
  **Woodcutting** adds melee knockback, and every other level of a melee weapon raises it too.
  **Smithing** and **Crafting** are the dear ones: up to 2 more weapon slots and 2 more passive
  slots.
  (The Dwarf engineer is out of the game for now; its cannon is a power-up. Saves keep it
  unlocked.)
- The **Armoury** (on the title screen) upgrades gear for good, with banked coins. Every piece
  that's come out of a casket is there; each upgrade takes it a step along its line, and from
  then on it comes out of every casket as what it's become. Most lines go up through better
  pieces, the real OSRS upgrades where there are any: an Amulet of strength becomes an Amulet of
  power, then of torture; a Fire cape an Infernal cape; Recipe for Disaster's gloves go mithril
  to barrows; Ranger boots become Pegasian boots. They end in a variant of the best piece with a
  new look and a quarter more of its stats: an ornament kit (Amulet of torture (or), Bandos
  chestplate (or)), an imbue (Berserker ring (i)), Twisted ancestral, Masori (f), the Avernic
  treads, the next tier of a diary cloak or ring. An upgrade costs 250 to 3,500 coins by the
  rarity of what it becomes, and an upgrade into a piece still locked behind a map task waits
  for it. Each piece has its own upgrades: upgrading the Amulet of strength leaves the Amulet of
  power as it was.
- **Quests** (on the title screen) pay banked coins: each is named for an OSRS quest and asks
  for kills (Goblin Diplomacy, 300 goblins), a boss (Vampyre Slayer, Count Draynor; Desert
  Treasure I, Fareed, Dessous and Damis) or caskets opened (Pirate's Treasure). They count over
  as many runs as they take, on any map but the fun ones, and each pays its coins once (100 to 2,500), when the
  run that finishes it ends; one finished mid-run rings the bell. The list is coloured like the
  client's quest tab: red until started, yellow while under way, green once done. In a run, a
  tracker under the minimap (and the pause menu) shows how far the map's quests have got.
- The **UI** grows with the window: it's laid out for one about 720 pixels tall, and drawn up
  to twice the size on bigger ones (about 1.35x in a 1080p browser window).
- **Sound**: every enemy, spell and pickup plays its OSRS sound effect, synthesized from the
  cache the way the client does it. **M** mutes; the volume is in the title screen's Settings and
  on the pause screen.
- **Camera spin**: the camera circles your character on the title screen and the menus; the
  title screen's Settings stop it (it starts off stopped if your system asks for reduced motion).

# How Run Escape plays

Everything in the game, map by map and system by system. The [README](../README.md) has the short
version and how to run it.

- **WASD** or the arrow keys to move. Weapons fire on their own. **1-4** use your utility items.
  **Esc** pauses. **Q** shows or hides the quest tracker. **Tab**, or clicking the DPS under the
  minimap, shows each weapon's damage a second and its total for the run. The mouse wheel zooms.
- On a touch screen, drag anywhere to move: the stick appears under your thumb. Tap the buttons on
  screen to pause and use items, with the thumb still on the stick if you like. Tap the DPS for each
  weapon's damage. Phones play sideways, with gear and quests in the pause menu.
- Your **revives** show beside your active effects, without a timer: a **Ring of life** from the
  shop, and the Pyromancer's own as a **Phoenix**. The phoenix is used first, and each icon goes
  once it's saved you. In co-op they're your own, not a teammate's.
- Enemies drop **bones**; walk near them to collect their experience. Bones lying close together
  gather into one pile worth all of their experience, and it looks the part as it grows: Big
  bones, Dragon bones, Wyvern bones, Lava dragon bones, then Superior dragon bones. Coins gather
  into bigger stacks the same way. With too many piles of either lying around, the one furthest
  away comes to the newest drop, so nothing left far behind is lost. Levels follow the OSRS experience table (stretched for the
  later levels), and each one offers a choice of three: a new weapon, a weapon upgrade or a
  passive. **1-4** pick with the keyboard. **R** rerolls the offer (one reroll a run, more from
  the Pirate and the shop), and **X** skips it for coins from the run: 25 the first time, then
  50, 100, 200 and 400, doubling each time.
- When a run ends, the results screen shows its stats: damage, kills and damage a second for
  each weapon (and the utility items and power-ups that did damage), the biggest hit, damage
  taken, hitpoints healed and when each boss went down. In co-op it has a page for each player,
  flipped through with the arrows or the arrow keys, and the party's shared totals under them. A
  weapon's damage a second counts up to when its player went down or left.
- You carry at most **4 weapons and 4 passives**, so a build is about what you leave out.
- **Rarity**, in OSRS drop table terms: every weapon and passive is Common, Uncommon, Rare or
  Very rare, which sets how often it's offered. Every offer also rolls its own rarity: a Common
  Piety adds 5% damage, a Very rare one 17.5%; rarer weapon upgrades add bonus damage to that
  weapon. Luck (Ring of wealth, the Slayer skill) makes rarer rolls likelier.
- **Weapons** upgrade the OSRS way: scimitars and 2h swords go bronze to dragon, Fire Strike
  becomes Bolt, Blast and Wave, throwing knives go bronze to rune, and shortbows go through the
  woods to magic while their arrows go through the metals. Thrownaxes go bronze to dragon, and
  each one bounces on from what it hits to the next enemy nearby, more times as they upgrade.
  Elemental runes circle you for a while, hitting what they pass and knocking it back, one more
  rune (air, water, earth, fire) every other level. A maxed scimitar with Piety evolves
  into the Abyssal whip; maxed Ice Burst with Protect from Melee becomes Ice Barrage. The weapon in your
  hand and passives like the Graceful outfit or Amulet of glory show up on your character.
- **The Ancient bursts**: Ice Burst freezes what's around you, Smoke Burst poisons it, Shadow
  Burst makes it take 20% more damage for a while and Blood Burst now and then heals you by 2.
  Level ups only offer Ice Burst. Beat the Desert and a burst's second level lets you pick which of
  the four you want, keeping its level, and it's your only burst for that run.
- **The keris partisans** can be offered to any adventurer: quick melee stabs that hit kalphites
  and scarabs (Kephri too) a third harder. The plain one is the Kalphite Queen's, and each jewel
  from the Tombs of Amascut's chest brings in a keris of its own. They're picked as the Ancient
  spells are: only the plain keris is offered on a level up, and at its level 2 you pick which
  it becomes, the plain
  one or a jewel's you've found, keeping its level (with no jewel found there's no pick). A jewel
  found before the Kalphite Queen's kill still brings the plain keris in, to take it. **Of
  breaching** now and then finds a gap in the shell for triple damage, far more often in
  kalphites and scarabs; **of corruption**'s every sixth stab is Wrath of Amascut, after which
  what it hit takes 25% more for 6 seconds; **of the sun** heals you on each kill, hits a quarter
  harder below a quarter of your hitpoints, and every fortieth stab is Tumeken's Light, healing
  you to full; and **of amascut** has all of it but Tumeken's Light. You carry one keris at most,
  so they can't be stacked. It's the one shared weapon that's Rare, so it comes with a Rare's
  bonus damage, and a ranged or magic adventurer who starts with a Common or Uncommon weapon holds
  the keris once they take it (the rarest weapon you carry is the one in your hand).
- **Evolutions** from the new maps' passives: maxed elemental runes with Vengeance become
  **Astral runes**, which never stop circling; maxed throwing knives with Obsidian armour become
  **Toktz-xil-ul**, obsidian rings that fly through everything; maxed Saradomin Strike with the
  Book of the dead becomes **Undead Grasp**, whose hands hold what they hit; and a maxed dragon 2h
  sword with Dizana's quiver becomes the **Colossal blade**, which hits bosses and elites again.
  The shortbow with **Rigour** becomes the **Dark bow**, the fire spells with **Augury**
  **Flames of Zamorak**, the thrownaxes with the Tome of fire **Chain-hit axes**, Blood Burst with
  Rapid Heal **Blood Barrage**, Smoke and Shadow Burst with the **Ancient sceptre** **Smoke** and
  **Shadow Barrage**, the Ivandis flail with the Book of the dead the **Blisterwood flail** and the
  Crystal bow with the Graceful outfit the **Corrupted bow**.
- **Class weapon evolutions**: the Scythe of Vitur with **Chivalry** becomes the **Sanguine Scythe
  of Vitur**, which sweeps wider and heals every fourth sweep; a godsword with **Piety** becomes the
  **Ancient godsword**, whose Blood Sacrifice hits all round and heals; the Dragon claws with the
  Tome of fire become the **Burning claws**; the Dragon warhammer with Protect from Melee becomes the
  **Elder maul**, whose Shockwave weakens everything around you; Dinh's bulwark with Vengeance becomes
  **Dinh's blazing bulwark**, whose Shield Bash reaches further and burns; the Twisted bow with Rigour
  becomes the **Corrupted twisted bow**; Tumeken's shadow with Augury becomes the **Corrupted
  tumeken's shadow**; the Sanguinesti staff with Rapid Heal becomes the **Holy sanguinesti staff**;
  and the Harmonised nightmare staff with the Amulet of glory becomes the **Eldritch nightmare staff**,
  whose surges split where they hit.
- **Evolving**: a weapon at its last level with its passive (at any level) offers its evolution first
  on every level up. An evolved weapon has three levels of its own and keeps the weapon's bonus
  damage. Most other class weapons, like the Toxic blowpipe and the Thunder khopesh, don't evolve.
  Level up cards say what evolves with what, the HUD and pause menu show how close a weapon is and
  its slot glows gold once it's ready, and the **Collection log** on the title screen lists every
  recipe, keeping what it makes as ??? until you've made it. It has a tab for each place with
  finds of its own (the Hallowed Night, the Theatre of Blood, the Tombs of Amascut, Justiciar
  Zachariah, Phosani's Nightmare): the weapons it brings in, then its trophies.
- **Style prayers**: **Chivalry** makes slashes and sweeps hit harder and adds max hitpoints for
  fighting up close, **Rigour** makes arrows, bolts and thrown weapons hit harder, and
  **Augury** makes spells hit harder and their effects last longer.
- **Adventurers**: three to start with, each with their own starting weapon, outfit and bonus.
  The rest join for beating a map or for a task done on one (in a single run, however it ends);
  the adventurer screen says what each one wants, and a task done mid-run rings the town
  crier's bell. One can be made a favourite (`SaveData.favouriteCharacter`), and the game starts
  on them.

- **Traits**: Act II's adventurers have a trait, a way to play, rather than a bonus. The
  **Forester**'s is **Timber!**: every 25 kills his next swing fells a Strangled Tree along the
  ground ahead of him, 8 tiles long, for four times the swing's damage, and his swings cut roots 2
  tiles further out than they reach. The **Shadow walker**'s is **Step between**: once every 30
  seconds, a hit that would take over a fifth of her hitpoints takes her into the shadows instead,
  a second and a half out of reach and 3 tiles on the way she faces; in the Lassar Undercity's
  city it takes her (and her party) into the Shadow Realm. The **Treasure hunter**'s is **Dig
  site**: a casket left on the ground buries itself after 3 seconds; walked over again from 20
  seconds it's a tier better, and at 60 seconds it's lost. The **Snow ranger**'s is **Shatter**:
  every fourth hit of hers chills what it hits for a second (at half its pace), and anything she
  kills while it's chilled or frozen bursts, its shards hitting everything within 3 tiles for a
  quarter of its hitpoints (a tenth of an elite's or a boss's). Under Ghorrock's cold she never
  slides on ice, and her cold rises 40% slower. The **Lantern-bearer**'s is **Lantern**: enemies
  within 3 tiles of her take 20% more from her hits and are 10% slower, and where it's dark her
  light's twice the size.
- **Class weapons**: every adventurer fights with Melee, Ranged or Magic, and only they can be
  offered their class's weapons. Each class has
  a Very rare one, offered in about one run in twenty, and Rare ones. A new weapon is always
  offered at its own rarity or better, so these come with bonus damage, and the rarest weapon
  you carry is the one in your hand.
    - **Melee**: the **Scythe of Vitur** sweeps a wide arc and hits bosses and elites three
      times (100%, 50%, 25%). The **godswords** are heavy two-handed sweeps, and every third
      swing that has something to hit is their special attack: Armadyl's The Judgement hits
      twice as hard, Bandos's Warstrike makes what it hits take 25% more damage, Saradomin's
      Healing Blade heals you by half the damage, and Zamorak's Ice Cleave freezes. You only
      carry one godsword, and the four share one weapon's chance of being offered. From the
      new maps: **Osmumten's fang**, fast stabs that hit bosses and elites again; the
      **Tzhaar-ket-om**, a slow sweep with huge knockback whose every fourth swing is a Quake
      that stuns; the **Dragon warhammer**, whose every third blow is a Smash that makes what it
      hits take 30% more damage; the **Inquisitor's mace**, crushing blows a quarter harder on the
      undead that hit bosses and elites again; the **Dual macuahuitl**, very fast slashes on both
      sides; the **Thunder khopesh**, quick slashes whose every third is a Thunderclap that hits
      everything around you; and **Dinh's bulwark**, a slow bash that hits everything around
      you, further out than any other melee weapon.
      The Godsword knight's **Godsword** is a bare blade until level 2, when you pick its hilt
      (Armadyl, Bandos, Saradomin or Zamorak) and with it its special attack. **Dragon claws**
      slash four times at once, the last the hardest, and the **Ivandis flail** hits vampyres and
      the undead 50% harder.
    - **Ranged**: the **Twisted bow** shoots the toughest enemy in range, and hits harder the
      more hitpoints it has (up to +150% on bosses). The **Bow of Faerdhinen** fires crystal
      arrows that fly on through what they hit. The **Toxic blowpipe** fires darts that leave
      venom in what they hit, hurting it every second; it goes for enemies without venom first
      (flying past the ones with it), so the venom spreads through a crowd. Each weapon level
      upgrades its darts: bronze, steel, mithril, rune, then dragon. One attack in 40 is its
      special, Toxic Siphon: darts that hit 50% harder and heal you for half what they deal, up
      to 3 hitpoints each. From the new maps:
      the **Dragon hunter crossbow**, dragon bolts that hit bosses and elites again, and the
      **Eclipse atlatl**, darts that fly through enemies and set them burning. The **Armadyl
      crossbow**'s bolts upgrade from bronze to steel, mithril, runite, then dragon. They go
      through what they hit and one in five is enchanted (a ruby takes a share of what's left of
      its hitpoints, a diamond pierces more, a dragonstone burns), and the **Crystal bow**'s
      arrows split into three where they first hit.
    - **Magic**: **Tumeken's shadow** fires splashing shadow blasts and triples your damage
      bonus for them. The **Harmonised nightmare staff** casts Fire Surge faster than anything
      else. The **Sanguinesti staff** casts blood blasts, and one hit in six heals you for half
      its damage, up to 2 hitpoints. From the new maps: the **Kodai wand**, Water Surge at two
      enemies at once, and the **Blue moon spear**, ice that crashes down and freezes. The
      **Crystal staff** grows from basic to attuned (level 4) and perfected (level 7), and
      **Toktz-mej-tal** pulses obsidian heat around you that leaves the ground burning. The
      **Volatile Nightmare staff** casts Immolate at the toughest enemy in range, slow and heavier
      the bigger the target. The **Nightmare staff**, Phosani's own, has her grasping claws come
      up out of the ground under enemies around you every few seconds, hands and all, with bosses
      and elites grabbed twice more.
- **Unlocks**: from the Desert on, every map holds new things to find. Beating it, killing one of
  its mid-run bosses, or finding something in a raid's chest, brings a weapon, a passive or a piece
  of gear into the game for every run after (a toast and the results screen say so). Until then it's
  never offered or dropped. Each map's card shows what it holds, faded until it's earned, and says
  on hover what to do for it. Everything from Lumbridge, the Barrows and the Wilderness is there
  from the start.

- **Fun maps**: the Grand Exchange, East Varrock, Falador, Last Man Standing, Castle Wars and Pest
  Control are open from the start and always endless. They have no final boss: a new round starts
  every fifteen minutes, tougher like an endless run's, and bosses come at random from each map's
  pool (the first at four minutes, then every three to five). Everyone there is another player,
  drawn in real gear (a few outfits per kind), and they say things over their heads; rangers and
  mages shoot, weakly. In Falador everyone plays Durial321, who answers back. Castle Wars has both
  teams, who fight each other too, and a Flag carrier who runs from you; Pest Control is the Void
  Knight's island and its pests, and its bosses are the four portals, which open together behind
  shields that drop one by one.
- **The Battle for Varrock** (on the Fun tab, its own row: a map to explore): Varrock as it is in
  OSRS, every house, shop, street, church and the palace where they stand, burnt to ruins. Walls
  charred, some fallen, some broken down (the Wilderness's ruined walls) and some still burning;
  roofs, doors and upstairs gone; breaches in the city wall, broken like Falador's crumbling
  wall; trees burnt, every bush and plant gone, the grass gone to ash; Gypsy Aris's tent and the
  market's stalls burning. Where the square's fountain stood is a
  crater, burning at its heart. Fires still burn, flames on their own and never a campfire, with
  burnt skeletons and the dead raiders of Phosani's Sanctuary and the Theatre of Blood where they
  fell. Nothing of them is in the way: you walk over the dead and through the fires, and a fire
  burns you as you do, a twentieth of your hitpoints at once and every half second you stay,
  through armour. The demons walk through them unharmed. The palace's floor is the floor
  system's tiles in an ashen skin, up its burnt front steps from its court, with Yama on his
  throne at its north end, and fires on it too. You start outside the south gate as
  Justiciar Zachariah, his own model, with his Saradomin sword (the 2h sword's sweep, and its
  blows land heavy: the ground shakes, and what it hits is knocked back further than a 2h sword's
  and staggers, held still a moment, though not a boss) and the
  moves of his fight on buttons (Space and 1 to 4, a pad's bottom button and shoulders, a cluster
  of buttons on a touch screen): Lunge, Shock wave, Ice barrage, Lightning and the Owl's gaze.
  The gaze is his ultimate, back every 30 seconds; the others come back in 5 to 14. He stands
  still while he casts, from the press to the strike, and rising floor can't hurt him mid-lunge:
  a lunge is a way through Yama's slabs. The
  Owl's gaze lights everything round him blue-white, and walls, ruins and pillars cast shadows it
  can't see into: anything in them is spared, and anything it judges is struck by Saradomin's
  lightning.
  It opens with a cinematic: close on Yama, who says resistance is futile; the camera pulls back
  to Sir Prysin and four Varrock guards before his throne; Prysin defies him, Yama answers
  "Pathetic." and snaps his fingers, and they burn away in Leagues IV's Trailblazer Reloaded
  death; then the camera flies south over the city to Zachariah and the run begins. Any key,
  click, tap or controller button skips it, and it plays each time the map's travelled to.
  Zachariah starts facing into the city. Its music is Sign Here, from Yama's domain, and
  Forged Through Fire takes over as Yama rises.
  Yama's demons fill its streets, the hordes of hell: imps at first as any map's first wave, then
  more and more, until from the eighth minute they're twice the crowd other maps' waves bring,
  coming twice as fast. Imps, reanimated demon spawn, hellhounds, lesser, revenant, greater and
  black demons, with an elite a minute from 100 seconds and rings of them now and then. There's no
  clock and no random events, and no items: no caskets, gear, power-ups, bag or weapons and
  passives to pick, only bones, coins and food (twice as often as elsewhere, a kill in about 170).
  Kill them for levels for as long as you like,
  though they get tougher by the minute. The first few levels come as anywhere, and then each
  takes more experience, up to six times as much from level 14: each level offers three blessings
  of Saradomin to choose from, and his strike comes down on you with the one you take (a reroll
  offers three more). Saradomin's strength
  (Might, which the Justiciar's buttons hit harder with too), a Saradomin brew (hitpoints, and a
  third of them back), Saradomin's ward (armour), Holy sandals (speed), a Holy elixir
  (4 hitpoints a minute), Saradomin's will (buttons back sooner, and the sword faster), a Holy book
  (experience), a Blessed blade (the Saradomin sword a level up), Saradomin bracers (the sword
  swings faster), a Saradomin platebody (its blows knock back further and stagger longer), a
  Saradomin staff (Saradomin Strike,
  then a level of it) and Zilyana's crossbow (from Saradomin's general, the Armadyl crossbow, then
  a level of it), and blessings on the buttons: Saradomin's light (all of them harder), Zeal (a
  longer, harder lunge), Saradomin's crozier (another shock wave either side), Holy water (a
  bigger ice barrage that holds longer), Saradomin's mjolnir (further, harder lightning) and
  Saradomin's halo (a wider, harder Owl's gaze). Each comes only so many times, and none is
  offered twice at once; once fewer than three are left, Saradomin's tears are offered beside
  them (a little Might, and half your hitpoints back), and then alone. They
  show in a row under the sword, and on the pause menu. Yama's two
  generals wait in their ruins from the start, to be fought in either order: step into one's ruin
  and it wakes, the demons about run from it every way, every way out bursts into fire, and you
  fight it there with nothing else coming. The timer stops while you do, and the hordes with it:
  they come back as they were when it woke. Neither is a fight for a fresh hero: on average the
  first wants about eight minutes of the hordes' levels, the second fifteen, and Yama no more.
  Die there and the run's over, as it is anywhere. Beat one and Saradomin heals you whole (and
  everyone still up in co-op), with the hordes still to come; and the other grows stronger: 50% more
